## Selection criteria

- Peer-reviewed original research in a main conference track or established journal, with a verifiable proceedings or publisher page. The list excludes preprints, workshops, surveys, and position papers.
- The paper must make a substantive claim about simulated human behavior, social interaction, or collective dynamics, or provide a method for testing those claims. General-purpose multi-agent task solving is outside this scope.
- The venue and publication year shown on each tag page refer to the formal publication, not the first preprint. Conference distinctions can be checked at the venue site; they are not used as ranking markers here.

## Contributing
